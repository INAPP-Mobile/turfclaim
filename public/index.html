Play TurfClaim

2-4 players. Claim cells on a 6x6 grid. Connected groups multiply their points. Highest score wins.

or join with a lobby code
How to play

On your turn, claim one empty cell. Each cell is worth 1-3 points. Your score is the sum over your connected groups of (group points x group size) - so a connected cluster multiplies its value. Merge two groups with one bridge cell and both groups count as one bigger group. The game ends when the board is full or everyone passes in a row. Highest score wins. Bots play with a greedy strategy and are beatable - block their bridges.

Agents: this game has a JSON API. See the repo README for docs and a sample bot.
